# Test na papiru: drugi dio (prirodni brojevi)

For Luka: the paper test for Part 2. It's from the brief's addendum (natural numbers), in the same four task types as Part 1's.

- **When:** give "Prije" before he opens Part 2 and "Poslije" when he finishes it. It takes about 10 minutes, with no hints and no calculator.
- **The numbers** appear in no lead. `src/content/leads/paper-test.test.ts` checks that for both versions, and fails if a lead ever uses one.
- **The Croatian** is my draft. Correct it here, as with the game's text.

---

## Prije

Ime: ______________________  Datum: ____________

**1. Usporedi bez računanja.**

a) Je li 73 · 58 + 27 · 58 jednako 100 · 58? Kako to znaš bez računanja?

b) Što je veće: 6 · 10³ + 9 · 10² ili 1 · 10⁴? Zašto?

**2. Predvidi.**

a) Jedan pribrojnik poveća se za 38, a drugi za 15. Što se dogodi sa zbrojem?

b) Što se promijeni ako u 48 : 6 · 2 stavimo zagradu oko 6 · 2?

**3. Pronađi pogrešku.**

a) Netko je napisao: „6 + 4 · 2 = 20”. Što je krivo i zašto?

b) Netko je napisao: „50 : 8 = 5, ostatak 10”. Što je krivo i zašto?

**4. U oba smjera.**

a) Smisli priču u kojoj se računa 4 · (6 + 3).

b) Napiši jedan izraz: u razredu je 12 dječaka i 15 djevojčica, a svatko ima 5 bilježnica. Koliko je bilježnica?

## Poslije

Ime: ______________________  Datum: ____________

**1. Usporedi bez računanja.**

a) Je li 64 · 37 + 36 · 37 jednako 100 · 37? Kako to znaš bez računanja?

b) Što je veće: 8 · 10³ + 7 · 10² ili 9 · 10³? Zašto?

**2. Predvidi.**

a) Jedan pribrojnik poveća se za 45, a drugi za 17. Što se dogodi sa zbrojem?

b) Što se promijeni ako u 36 : 3 · 2 stavimo zagradu oko 3 · 2?

**3. Pronađi pogrešku.**

a) Netko je napisao: „5 + 3 · 4 = 32”. Što je krivo i zašto?

b) Netko je napisao: „60 : 7 = 7, ostatak 11”. Što je krivo i zašto?

**4. U oba smjera.**

a) Smisli priču u kojoj se računa 3 · (5 + 4).

b) Napiši jedan izraz: u razredu je 14 dječaka i 13 djevojčica, a svatko ima 4 olovke. Koliko je olovaka?

## For you: what each task shows

| Task | Understood | Memorised, or guessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | "73 and 27 make 100, so it's 100 groups of 58" (the distributive law, which the brackets locks teach) | Calculates both sides |
| 1b | Compares place values: 6 thousands and 9 hundreds is under 10 thousands | Counts zeros, or calculates |
| 2a | The sum grows by 38 + 15 = 53, whatever the addends are | Asks for the numbers |
| 2b | Without brackets it's (48 : 6) · 2 = 16; with them it's 48 : 12 = 4. Division and multiplication go left to right | Says nothing changes |
| 3a | Multiplication comes first: 6 + 8 = 14. Someone added first | Only gives 14, with no reason |
| 3b | The remainder must be smaller than 8: 50 : 8 = 6, remainder 2 | Only checks 8 · 5 + 10 = 50, which is true |
| 4a | A story with groups of a sum: 4 bags with 6 apples and 3 pears each | A story for 4 · 6 + 3 |
| 4b | (12 + 15) · 5, or 12 · 5 + 15 · 5 | 12 + 15 · 5 |

"Poslije" has the same answers on its own numbers:

- 1b: 8 700 against 9 000.
- 2a: the sum grows by 62.
- 2b: 24 against 6.
- 3a: 17.
- 3b: 8, remainder 4.

Part 2 worked if he does better after than before on at least 3 of the 4 tasks (GDD, Success signals).
